Good to know

Frequently asked

Can you drill on a small or tight property?+

Yes. Our range of rigs lets us access properties with limited access and difficult terrain, and we specialise in drilling and installation in confined spaces. Borehole drilling isn't only for large-scale projects.

What is Odex drilling?+

Overburden Drilling Extension (ODEX) combines casing and drilling at the same time, giving a stable, precise borehole in challenging soil and rock conditions.

Can you fix an old or weak borehole?+

Our borehole cleaning and rehabilitation restores underperforming boreholes through professional flushing and maintenance, and we repair damaged pumps, clogged pipes and reduced water flow.

What happens when the power goes off?+

We install borehole backup systems — generators, solar-powered systems and UPS units — so your water keeps flowing during power failures.

Which areas do you cover?+

Gauteng, North West, Limpopo, Mpumalanga and the Free State.
